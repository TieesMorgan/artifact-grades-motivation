01 • Institutional Habit

The "Grammar of Schooling"

Treated as an unquestioned constant (Tyack & Cuban, 1995, as cited in Schneider & Hutt, 2014), letter grades feel like pure measures of learning—yet actually function as administrative tools.

02 • Antiquity – Renaissance

Oral Disputation Before Numbers

Classical and medieval education evaluated mastery through live dialogue and public defense—fostering reasoning without rankings or letter marks (Williams, 2022).

03 • 18th–19th Centuries

The Administrative Sorting Machine

Born to manage records and sort graduates for expanding labor markets, high-stakes A–F scales turn curious students into risk-averse “entrepreneurial learners” (Schneider & Hutt, 2014).

04 • Composite Variance & Equity

75% Non-Cognitive & Bias Distortion

Three-quarters of GPA variance reflects compliance and subjective bias—disproportionately penalizing neurodivergent processing styles despite strong mastery (Baniasadi et al., 2023; Brookhart et al., 2016).

05 • Lifelong Competence

Informational Feedback Exit Ramps

Stripping away controlling marks lets criteria-referenced feedback and low-stakes practice build autonomy, self-regulation, and genuine growth (Arhavbarien, 2026; Ryan & Deci, 2020).
